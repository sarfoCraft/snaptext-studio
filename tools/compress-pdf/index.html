    If a scanned PDF is too large to attach to an email or upload to a form, try Compress PDF. The converter renders each page into a compressed image and rebuilds a new PDF using your chosen quality level.

    How to use Compress PDF

    1. Choose the PDF document.
    2. Select a JPEG quality level using the slider.
    3. Click Compress PDF and allow the pages to render.
    4. Check the downloaded file size and verify that text remains readable.

    Important compression trade-offs

    This workflow rasterizes pages before saving them. It can shrink some scanned or image-heavy PDFs, but it may produce larger files for already optimized PDFs.

    Text may become non-selectable, links and form fields may not survive, and small writing can lose sharpness. Keep your original document and inspect the output before relying on it.

    Choosing a quality level

    A lower setting prioritizes smaller images and can reduce clarity. A higher setting tends to preserve more visual detail. If the file contains small text or diagrams, begin with higher quality and compare results.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will compression always make a PDF smaller?

    No. PDFs that are already efficient may stay similar in size or become larger.

    Will the text remain selectable?

    Usually not. This converter recreates pages as images, so selectable text and interactive elements may be lost.
