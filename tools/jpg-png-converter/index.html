    Which image format should you choose?

    PNG supports transparent backgrounds and is useful for logos, screenshots and graphics with sharp edges. JPG generally gives smaller photo files but does not support transparency.

    When converting a transparent PNG to JPG, transparent areas are filled with white in this tool. Converting a JPG to PNG does not magically restore details previously lost by JPEG compression.
