    Use the color-based background remover for photographs or graphics captured against a consistent white or plain background. Click the background color in the preview, adjust the tolerance and export a transparent PNG.

    How to use Background Remover

    1. Upload an image with a plain background.
    2. Click a background area on the image preview to sample its color.
    3. Adjust the tolerance slider to include more or fewer similar pixels.
    4. Click Remove background to download the transparent PNG.

    Best results come from simple backgrounds

    This tool removes pixels that resemble your sampled background color. It works best when the subject and background have clearly different colors.

    It is not an AI segmentation model. Fine hair, shadows, multi-colored scenes and objects with colors close to the sampled background may need a more advanced editor.

    Understanding color tolerance

    Increasing tolerance removes a wider range of similar colors, which can clean up uneven backgrounds. Too much tolerance may accidentally erase parts of the subject, so adjust conservatively.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does this work with detailed or busy backgrounds?

    Not reliably. It is designed for plain or nearly solid-color backgrounds.

    What output format do I get?

    The tool downloads a PNG image containing transparency.
