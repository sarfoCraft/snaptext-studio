    Remove unwanted margins, desks and surrounding areas from a document photo before scanning its text. Drag a crop frame over the section you want to keep and send the cropped image to SnapText Studio's OCR workspace.

    How to use Scanner with Crop

    1. Choose a document image or photograph.
    2. Move and resize the crop handles to frame the page or text area.
    3. Click Crop & send to OCR.
    4. Continue with text extraction in the main OCR workspace.

    Why crop before OCR?

    Cropping can help the recognition engine focus on text by removing irrelevant objects and blank surroundings. It is especially useful for phone photographs where the document occupies only part of the image.

    Keep all lines inside the crop boundary. If a page is skewed, this tool provides a rectangular crop rather than full perspective correction.

    After cropping

    The cropped image is handed to the OCR workspace in your browser so you can select a language, extract text and export it. This workflow does not automatically save a separate image crop file.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I crop a photo from my phone?

    Yes. Upload the image and adjust the crop area with the handles.

    Does it straighten perspective automatically?

    No. The current tool provides rectangular cropping, not automatic perspective correction.
