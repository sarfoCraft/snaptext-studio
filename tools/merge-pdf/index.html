    Merge several PDF documents into one file without copying and pasting pages manually. Select your source PDFs, review their order, and let the tool combine their pages into a new PDF.

    How to use Merge PDFs

    1. Upload at least two PDF files.
    2. Rearrange the file order using the list controls.
    3. Click Merge files to combine their pages.
    4. Download your merged PDF and check the final page order.

    Keep your documents in a sensible sequence

    Merging is useful for combining reports, application attachments, invoices or project handouts. Put the cover or first document at the top of the list, then add supporting files underneath.

    The tool appends complete documents. It does not automatically identify duplicate pages, remove blank pages or insert page numbers.

    PDF compatibility

    Most ordinary PDFs can be merged directly, preserving the page contents. Password-protected or damaged PDFs may not load until their restrictions are removed by the document owner.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I reorder PDFs before merging?

    Yes. The selected-file list includes arrow controls to move a PDF earlier or later.

    Does merging change the original PDF files?

    No. The tool produces a new combined file for download.

    How many PDFs can I merge?

    At least two files are required. The practical upper limit depends on browser memory and the sizes of your PDFs.
