Convert between JPG and PNG formats

The image converter changes supported image files between PNG and JPG. It is useful when a website, document, design tool or upload form requires a particular image format. You can select multiple images in one session.

How to convert images

  1. Choose one or more image files.
  2. Select PNG or JPG as the output format.
  3. Click Convert images.
  4. Download the converted image or ZIP archive for a batch.

PNG or JPG: which format should you choose?

PNG is useful for crisp graphics, screenshots and images that need transparency. JPG is commonly used for photographs and often produces smaller files, but it does not support transparency. When a transparent PNG is converted to JPG, transparent areas are filled with white.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert several images at once?

Yes. Select multiple images and the tool bundles the converted files into a ZIP archive.

Does JPG support transparent backgrounds?

No. Transparent areas become white when converting to JPG.
