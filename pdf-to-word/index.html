Convert a PDF into an editable Word document

Use this PDF to Word converter when you need to edit text from a PDF in Microsoft Word or another DOCX-compatible editor. WordToPDF extracts selectable PDF text and provides an OCR language setting for scanned pages. The exported document may need formatting adjustments, especially when the original PDF contains columns, tables, forms or unusual fonts.

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Select Choose PDF and pick a PDF file from your device.
  2. For scanned pages, choose the recognition language that matches the document.
  3. Select Convert to DOCX and wait for the conversion to finish.
  4. Open the downloaded DOCX file in Word or a compatible editor.
  5. Check headings, page breaks, tables, symbols and numbers before sharing the document.

Text PDFs and scanned PDFs are different

A text-based PDF contains selectable characters, so a converter can usually extract its text directly. A scanned PDF may contain page images instead of a text layer. OCR is needed to recognise the words in those images. OCR can make scanned content editable, but it may misread small print, handwriting, low-resolution scans or complex layouts.

What to check after conversion

  • Compare names, dates, amounts and other critical details with the source PDF.
  • Review page order, headings, tables and bullet lists.
  • Check Urdu or other right-to-left text carefully if present.
  • Keep the original PDF as a reference until the DOCX has been reviewed.

Frequently asked questions

Does PDF to Word preserve the exact layout?

Not always. PDF and Word use different layout models. Simple text documents usually convert more cleanly than forms, multi-column pages and highly designed documents.

Can I convert a scanned PDF?

Scanned pages require OCR. Choose the correct OCR language and proofread the result carefully.

Is DOCX the same as DOC?

DOCX is the modern Office Open XML Word document format. It is supported by current versions of Microsoft Word and many compatible editors.
