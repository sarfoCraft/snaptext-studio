Remove a plain background from an image

This background remover is designed for images with a plain, single-colour background such as a white product photo or a simple graphic. You sample the background colour, adjust tolerance and export a PNG with transparent areas.

How to use the background remover

  1. Choose an image with a simple background.
  2. Click the background area in the preview to sample its colour.
  3. Adjust colour tolerance carefully. A higher value removes a wider range of similar colours.
  4. Review edges and details, then export the transparent PNG.

Know the limitations

This is a colour-based background removal tool, not an AI subject-segmentation system. It works best with flat, uniform backgrounds. It may not cleanly separate hair, fur, transparent objects, shadows or subjects against busy scenes. For complex images, use a dedicated AI background-removal service and inspect its privacy and output terms.

Frequently asked questions

Can it remove a background from any photo?

No. It is intended for plain, single-colour backgrounds. Complex scenes may produce rough edges or remove parts of the subject.

Which format keeps transparency?

PNG supports transparency and is the intended export format for this tool.
