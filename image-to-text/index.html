Turn screenshots and photos into editable text

WordToPDF's Image to Text (OCR) workspace helps you copy words from screenshots, photographs and scanned pages without retyping them. Add an image, choose a recognition language, run OCR, then review and edit the extracted text before exporting it.

How to extract text from an image

  1. Choose an image file, drag it into the upload area, or paste a screenshot.
  2. Select the language used in the image. English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi and several other languages are available.
  3. Select Extract text from image and wait for recognition to finish.
  4. Correct names, numbers, punctuation and line breaks in the editor.
  5. Copy the result, download a TXT file, or export it as a PDF.

When OCR is useful

OCR (optical character recognition) is useful when text is visible in an image but cannot be selected like normal document text. Common examples include lecture slides, receipts, printed forms, screenshots, photographed notes and scanned pages. Results depend on image quality, language, font, contrast and page layout. Always proofread important information after recognition.

Privacy and processing

The current WordToPDF OCR workflow is designed to process the selected image in your browser rather than upload it to a WordToPDF application server. The page may load OCR software and language data from third-party content delivery networks. For confidential documents, review the site's privacy information and your organisation's data rules before use.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert a screenshot to editable text?

Yes. Upload or paste a screenshot, choose its language and run OCR. You can edit and copy the result.

Does OCR always produce perfect text?

No. Blurry images, handwriting, unusual fonts, low contrast and complex layouts can reduce accuracy. Check the output before relying on it.

Can I use Urdu OCR?

The language selector includes Urdu and English plus Urdu. Choose the option that best matches the text in your image.
