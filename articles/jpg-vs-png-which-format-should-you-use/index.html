JPG works well for photographs

JPEG uses lossy compression and can be efficient for detailed photos. Very low quality settings may produce visible artifacts around edges and text.

PNG is a good fit for graphics

PNG is lossless and supports transparency, making it useful for screenshots, logos and design elements. Large photos saved as PNG can use substantially more space.

Changing formats

Convert images based on the format required by your website, printer or document workflow. Format conversion cannot restore details already lost from a compressed original.
