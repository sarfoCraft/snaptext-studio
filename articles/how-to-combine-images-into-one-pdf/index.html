How to Combine Multiple Images into One PDF

A quick walkthrough for preparing document photos, ordering pages and exporting one convenient PDF.

Prepare your image files

Make sure images are legible, upright and cropped to show the relevant content. Photos taken under natural lighting usually reproduce text more clearly than blurry captures.

Arrange your pages

Choose your images and confirm they are in the correct sequence. Use the ordering controls to move cover pages or supporting documents before exporting.

Create and check your PDF

Run Images to PDF, download the combined file and check every page before sharing. Each image is fitted to an A4 page by this tool.
