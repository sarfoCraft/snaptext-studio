How to Scan Documents to PDF and Combine Multiple Pages

Learn how to scan paper pages with a phone, save the scans and combine supported images into one PDF. This guide explains what WordToPDF can and cannot do.

WordToPDF Guides · Published November 28, 2026 · Updated November 28, 2026

Open Images to PDF ↗

Choose the right way to scan a paper document

You can scan paper documents with a phone's built-in document scanner, a scanning app or a desktop scanner. These tools capture the page and may save it directly as a PDF. If you already have one PDF, you do not need to turn it into images just to keep the pages together.

If you photograph pages with the regular camera instead, use even lighting, keep the phone parallel to the paper and make sure every line is in focus. Save each page as a supported image file if you plan to combine the photos with WordToPDF.

How to scan multiple pages into one PDF on a phone

On iPhone

  1. Open a document-scanning feature on your iPhone, such as the scanner in Notes.
  2. Capture each page in sequence and review the edges before saving.
  3. Save the scanned document as a PDF in Files or share it to the location you need.

On Android

  1. Open the document scanner built into your phone or a scanning app you trust.
  2. Capture each page, add the next page to the same scan and review the order.
  3. Save or export the multi-page scan as a PDF. Menu names vary by device and app version.

These phone scanners create the scan. WordToPDF does not use the camera to scan paper directly.

Combine scanned image files with WordToPDF

If your pages are saved as separate JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP or BMP images, use the Images to PDF converter to place them in one PDF.

  1. Open Images to PDF in your browser.
  2. Select the page images in the order you want them to appear.
  3. Choose Create PDF and wait for the browser to build the document.
  4. Download the result and check every page before sharing it.

The current tool accepts up to 50 images per batch, with a maximum of 20 MB per image. Each image is fitted onto an A4 page while keeping its proportions. It does not add an OCR text layer, so text in the image pages is not automatically searchable.

What if you already have scanned PDF files?

If your phone scanner has already created multiple PDF files, use the Merge PDF tool to combine them. The merge tool accepts 2 to 20 PDFs, up to 50 MB each. It joins existing PDF documents; it does not combine loose image files.

If you need to recognize words from a photograph or screenshot, use Image to Text OCR. If a document photo includes a desk or empty borders, the document crop tool can prepare the image before OCR.

Frequently asked questions about scanning documents to PDF

How do I scan several pages as one PDF?

Use a phone or scanner app that supports multi-page scanning, capture the pages in order and save them as one PDF. If you have separate image files instead, combine them with WordToPDF Images to PDF.

How do I scan all my documents into one PDF?

Scan related pages in groups, check their order and save each group as a PDF. If the pages are separate images, select the supported images together in Images to PDF. Keep the number and size of files within the tool's stated limits.

Does WordToPDF scan paper documents?

No. WordToPDF can combine image files into a PDF and crop document images before OCR. Use your phone's scanner or a scanning app to capture paper pages.

Will the image-based PDF have searchable text?

Not automatically. Images to PDF places the images on PDF pages. Use OCR to extract text separately, and proofread the result.
